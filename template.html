Data.

How the numbers are worked out. Total Sales is the sum of Total_Sales_INR for every order in view, whatever its status. Total Profit is the sum of Profit_INR. Orders is the count of Order_ID. Units Sold is the sum of Quantity. Average Order Value is Total Sales divided by Orders. Profit Margin is Total Profit divided by Total Sales. Return Rate and Cancellation Rate are returned or cancelled orders divided by all orders. Revenue Lost is the sales value of Cancelled and Returned orders.

Please note. In the source file, Cancelled and Returned orders keep their sales value but carry zero profit. They are therefore included in Total Sales and shown separately as Revenue Lost, and they pull the profit margin down.
